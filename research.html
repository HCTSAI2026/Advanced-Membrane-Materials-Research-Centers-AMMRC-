AMMRC RESEARCH

Research

Six integrated research themes connect advanced membrane materials with molecular transport, environmental purification, energy conversion, interface engineering and smart functional systems.

01

Advanced Separation Membranes

Design of polymeric, nanocomposite and mixed-matrix membranes for nanofiltration, pervaporation, gas separation, desalination and responsive molecular transport.

02

Water & Environmental Technologies

Membrane-enabled water purification, contaminant removal, solar-driven evaporation and resource recovery for sustainable environmental applications.

03

CO₂ Capture & Molecular Transport

Selective gas transport and carbon capture through polymeric, porous and mixed-matrix membrane platforms with tailored molecular-sieving pathways.

04

Energy & Ionic Transport Membranes

Ion-conducting membranes and electrochemical materials for fuel cells, batteries, redox-flow systems, electrolyzers and clean-energy conversion.

05

Membrane Interface & Surface Engineering

Interfacial polymerization, surface grafting, pore/interface tuning and anti-fouling strategies to improve membrane selectivity, stability and transport performance.

06

Optoelectronic & Smart Membrane Materials

Integration of conductive, photonic, electroactive and low-dimensional materials into membrane architectures for sensing, actuation and smart transport.
